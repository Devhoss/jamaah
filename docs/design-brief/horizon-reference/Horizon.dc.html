How it works
  1. Your phone’s GPS works without a network, even in airplane mode.
  2. Jama’ah works out the prayer times for where the aircraft is, and updates them as it moves.
  3. Times further along are estimates: the speed and route can change.
  4. The Qibla is shown against the aircraft’s nose. Enter the heading from the seat-back map; the phone’s compass is not reliable inside an aircraft.
