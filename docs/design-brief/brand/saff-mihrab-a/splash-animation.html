Splash, compressed to the one-second cut

The prototype’s splash ran 1 420 ms — Android 12 stops the system splash at about one second. Compressed, the arch draws in 400 ms, the row gathers 40 ms apart, and you step in last, landing exactly on the cut. Flip between the shipped speed and the prototype to see what the compression costs.

What was compressed: arch draw 520 → 400 ms · row stagger 60 → 40 ms · figure pop 380 → 260 ms · your entry starts 800 → 620 ms and runs 620 → 380 ms. Every easing curve is kept — the order (arch, row, you last) is the story, and it survives intact.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the splash is shown at its final frame — the same way the app honours it.

Ship note. Android 12 shows only the icon for this window (~1 s, then it holds the last frame until Flutter’s first frame replaces it) — the wordmark underneath comes from the app’s first frame, not the OS splash. Native route: an animated-vector with trimPathEnd on the arch and alpha/translate on five groups, ending ≤ 1 000 ms, driven by splash_icon.xml regenerated in make_brand.py.
